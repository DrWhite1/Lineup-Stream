# Lineup Stream

An independent GPL-3.0 fork of [IPVIO Stream](https://github.com/Emrodev/IPVIO-Stream) by its contributors. Not an official IPVIO release and not endorsed by upstream. Original code, font licenses and third-party notices remain in this source tree. See LICENSE.

## Fork changes (0.2.0)

- Named lineups open to synced groups, not a flattened dump of all their channels.
- Choose a provider category in Live TV, hold OK/menu, choose **Add synced group to lineup**, then an existing lineup or **New lineup**.
- Open the lineup in the Live TV sidebar, choose a group, then browse its channels with the existing now/next guide and programme panel.
- Individual channels are kept under a separate **Individual channels** entry using upstream's channel menus.
- Hold a synced group entry to remove that rule. It does not delete provider channels.
- Xtream uses player_api.php directly and keeps the provider's category IDs through parsing and cache. Added/removed channels follow refreshes; category renames keep membership if the ID is unchanged. M3U inputs lacking category IDs fall back to source+group-name identity and need reselection after a rename.
- The same category ID from different provider server/user identities does not mix. Group rule identity does not include the password. Server/username changes count as a different source.
- New app identity, icon/banner and updater addresses. May be installed beside official IPVIO and the earlier Lineup TV prototype. Their settings are separate and are not silently migrated.
- No VPN service or VPN subscription required.

## Limits and testing

This is an experimental personal build. Provider VOD compatibility, TV remote focus, guide availability and hardware decoding still need real-device testing. Selecting a group limits UI work; initial catalog import still has to read provider data and is not promised to be instant. Guide data is provided by the subscription, not generated by the app.

The upstream is feature-rich, not minimal: global state, a large single-module codebase, streaming catalogs and custom caches. The fork adds a small scoped feature rather than rewriting its navigation. Upstream's optional phone import uses HTTP on your local network. Do not use that credential-entry feature on untrusted Wi-Fi. Other inherited security issues are recorded in docs/SECURITY.md; those are not claimed fixed by this lineup change.
